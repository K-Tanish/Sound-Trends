Key Business Insights
UNIT VI
↑ Energy
Energy & loudness are the strongest correlated pair (r ≈ 0.76), confirming modern production drives perceived intensity.
Classical ≠ Pop
Classical sits as a fully isolated cluster in PCA space — the most acoustically distinct genre with lowest energy (0.20) and highest acousticness (0.92).
Dance ≠ Popular
Danceability has a near-zero correlation with popularity. Metal, despite low danceability, scores the highest avg popularity at 56.4.
Hip-hop explicit
Hip-hop has the highest explicit content rate. Yet explicit tracks, counterintuitively, do not significantly outperform clean tracks in popularity.
